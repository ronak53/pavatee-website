Paper receipts are the default nobody wants

71% of consumers say they prefer digital receipts, yet actual adoption sits at only about 27% across sectors. The gap exists because the only real path to digital receipts today requires integrating with the POS software itself — something independent retailers on older or simpler systems can't do, or can't justify.

Thermal receipt paper is also a real ongoing cost, in a market growing roughly 9% annually, and commonly contains BPA. And even where digital receipts are offered today, customers are typically asked to type their real email or phone number into the POS, handing their contact information straight into the retailer's marketing system.

One device, installed inline — zero POS changes

Pavatee installs inline between a store's POS and its receipt printer — network, USB, or serial, whichever the store already has — with no POS software changes required. It holds each receipt for 60 seconds: the customer taps their phone or an NFC card to go paperless, the cashier can force an immediate print, and it auto-prints if nobody acts, so nothing is ever lost.

Because it works at the printer's own protocol instead of through POS software, it works with any POS — and because it never asks the customer for contact information, the retailer never captures an email or phone number just to offer a digital receipt. Installation requires no reconfiguration of the POS or printer.

How it works

1

Tap

The customer taps their phone or an NFC card on the device. The receipt goes digital — nothing prints on paper.

2

Button

The cashier can press the button on the device at any time to print the receipt immediately, exactly as it works today.

3

Auto-print

If nobody acts within 60 seconds, the receipt prints automatically. A receipt is never lost — worst case, it just prints like it always did.

Works with any POS — because it doesn't touch your POS

Every existing competitor — Slip, refive, ReceiptHero, TapReceipt, Receiptless — integrates at the POS software layer, which means each one depends on POS vendor cooperation and can't serve retailers on older or simpler systems. Pavatee works one layer below, at the printer protocol itself, so it works with any POS regardless of brand or age.

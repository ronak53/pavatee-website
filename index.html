Built for independent stores

Keep your POS. Keep your printer.

Many digital-receipt tools connect through your POS software. Pavatee works at the printer instead, so it does not depend on which POS you run.

  • One cable to move. It installs between your POS and your printer.
  • Same receipt. The customer sees the same logo and layout that would have printed.
  • Private by design. No email or phone number asked. Receipts are deleted after 30 days.
  • Less paper to buy. A receipt that never prints is a receipt you never pay for. Thermal paper often contains BPA, which digital receipts avoid.

Questions

What store owners ask first.

Do customers need an app?

No. They tap their phone on the plate and the receipt opens in the browser. It works on Android and iPhone, with no sign-in.

Will it work with my POS?

It connects at the printer, not inside your POS software, so it does not depend on which POS you use. We check your printer model before installing.

What if a customer wants a paper receipt?

The cashier presses one button on the device and the receipt prints on your normal printer.

What happens to the receipt data?

We do not ask customers for an email address or phone number. Receipts are deleted after 30 days.

How much does it cost?

We are setting pricing together with the first pilot stores. Send us a message and we will tell you where it stands.

Who is behind Pavatee?

Pavatee is built in Halifax, Nova Scotia, by a software developer working with local independent stores.

Want it in your store?

We are running pilots with independent stores in Halifax and Bedford. Tell us a little about your store and we will get back to you to set up a short call.
